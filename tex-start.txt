\section*{Introduction}

This book contains useful knowledge for students studying for a course in
Machine Learning, or for anyone interested in this magical subject. The
structure of the book is the result of my notes during the "Introduction to
Machine Learning" course held by Elisa Ricci at the University of Trento in the
academic year 2024-2025, and cross referencing them with the book "Pattern
Recognition and Machine Learning" by Christopher Bishop. A later revision is in
the work while following the Machine Learning course at the university of
Camerino held by Michele Loreti in the year 2026-2027. During the course I found
myself more interested in the math than necessary, so I dedicated some of my
time into developing a better understanding of the theory and to fill some of
the gaps in my knowledge. The book you are reading is the result of this work.

Permission is granded to redistribute this content freely. The author authorizes
everyone to freely download, share, modify, contribute and publish this book
through any medium, shape or form: access to knowledge \textbf{MUST} be free and
accessible to whoever seeks to understand. The latest and greatest version of
this document can be found in the main branch of the github repo
\href{https://github.com/San7o/Introduction-to-machine-learning}{https://github.com/San7o/Introduction-to-machine-learning}.

By reading this book, you will gain an understanding of this wizardry that
machine learning is, and get to know a few spells and party tricks to impress
venture capitalists and imaginary friends (or your AI girlfriend now). Buckle up
and enjoy the ride!
